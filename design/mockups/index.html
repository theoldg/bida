The core loop

Open a group, add what you spent, see who owes whom. Everything else is secondary to these four screens.

The three things Tricount won't do

Receipts by the handful, a complete edit history you can roll back, and a personal lens over the group's ledger.

Why a highlighter

Personal mode can't use the red/green columns — those already mean debit and credit. A translucent amber wash reads as something laid over the ledger rather than part of it, which is exactly what a personal lens is. It also survives colour-blindness, because it comes with a left edge bar and an explicit "you: €14,28" figure.

Numbers are monospaced, always

Every figure is IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures, so decimal points line up down a column and you can eyeball a total without reading it. Text stays in Karla. The two never mix inside one line.

Colour is never the only signal

Debit and credit always carry a sign, a word ("you owe" / "you're owed") and a bar direction. Pending sync carries a dot and a banner. Nothing in the app is legible only to people who can separate red from green.

Built on shadcn

Every component above maps to a shadcn/ui primitive with these tokens swapped into its CSS variables — Sheet for the split editor, Drawer for add-expense, Tabs, Avatar, Badge, Dialog. Nothing here needs a custom widget except the keypad and the balance bar.

Open, before I build

None of these block the design — redline the screens and answer these whenever.

Comma or point for decimals?Mocked as European (€57,10). Trivial to flip, but it sets the tone of the whole app.

Should a restore be undoable by anyone, or only by whoever made the change?Link-only groups mean there's no real identity behind a member, so my instinct is: anyone can restore anything, and history makes it obvious who did. Cheap to change later.

Categories — fixed list or free-form?Mocked as a small fixed set with an emoji. A free-form tag field is friendlier but makes later AI categorisation and any spend breakdown much messier.

Is personal mode a toggle or the default?Mocked as a toggle that remembers itself. Making it the default reads better solo, worse when you hand your phone to someone at dinner.
